About this demo

Small-business insurance applications are self-declared, and answers like "serves alcohol" or "open after midnight" are often wrong. This demo checks those answers for Manhattan restaurants against real public records (NYC health inspections, NYS liquor licences, Dining Out licences, the NY company register, FDNY, DOB, PLUTO, 311 and OpenStreetMap) and each business's own website, and shows the evidence behind every fact.

We downloaded these sources, matched businesses across them, and used Gemini to read business websites. Gemini must quote the supporting sentence word for word, and we discard any answer whose quote isn't on the page. Facts are resolved by source trust, and where sources disagree the app flags a conflict: those are the questions an underwriter should ask. Google Maps reviews are shown live only and never stored.

Read the PRD (PDF) for sources, method and success metrics.
