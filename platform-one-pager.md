# Anaira Underwriting Control: Commercial Property

**Check every commitment against the evidence and your own rules before you make it, whoever writes the business.**

---

### The problem

- Property rates are falling (U.S. −13%, cat-exposed −20% in Q2 2026), so there's no cushion left to absorb stale values, weak terms or quiet rate erosion.
- The evidence for each decision sits in a dozen places: policy system, rater, claims, engineering, cat models, guidelines, broker emails, coverholder bordereaux. People rebuild it by hand, often too late.
- Leakage goes unseen until a loss:
  - values that never moved
  - deductibles below today's guidelines
  - a binder that doesn't match the issued policy
  - referrals nobody made
  - MGAs writing outside their authority

### How it works

1. **Reads** your existing systems and documents: policy, claims, rater, engineering, cat, guidelines, submissions, binding authority agreements, bordereaux.
2. **Reconciles** them into one evidence record, noting where sources disagree.
3. **Checks** exposure, price, terms, appetite and authority against your own rules.
4. **Quantifies** each finding in dollars.
5. **Routes** it to the right person, with referral memos and coverholder queries pre-filled.

### What it's not

- It doesn't replace your policy system, rater, cat model, workbench, intake tool or bordereau service. It sits beside them and reads their output.
- It doesn't make decisions. A named person decides; every recommendation is accepted or rejected with a reason.
- It doesn't send your data anywhere else. It's deployed in your own cloud and never shared across carriers.
